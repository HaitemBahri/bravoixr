# Media

The decided art direction for decorative artwork — illustrations, empty states, decorative
backgrounds, and hero/spot graphics. Like `feel`, this is an **identity-only** category: it has no
token layer of its own, because its colors *are* the existing primary (blue) and secondary (teal)
color primitives. Every other rule here is a stance, not a measurable value.

Each decision lists the **decided value** and a one-line **why**.

## Scope

- **Covers decorative / illustrative artwork** — illustrations, empty-state graphics, decorative
  backgrounds, and hero / spot graphics.
  _Why: these are the surfaces where the brand gets to be expressive beyond UI chrome._
- **Excludes functional icons** (the `icons` category) **and content photography** (user- or
  app-supplied images).
  _Why: icons are a governed measurable system, and photography carries real-world color this art
  direction can't dictate._

## Color rule

- **All artwork is built from the primary (blue) + secondary (teal) palettes only** — the figure /
  subject uses no other hue.
  _Why: a two-hue artwork language keeps every illustration unmistakably on-brand and coherent._
- **Flat fills, no gradients** — artwork uses solid blue / teal at chosen ramp steps; the signature
  blue→teal gradient from `color.md` is **not** used in artwork.
  _Why: flat fills keep illustrations calm and uniform; gradients stay reserved for UI surfaces so
  they keep their accent meaning._
- **Neutrals and the canvas color are allowed for ground, lines, and subtle detail** — but never as
  the figure's primary color.
  _Why: a neutral ground gives art depth and breathing room while blue + teal stay the subject._

## Style stance

- **Geometric, softly rounded** — artwork is constructed from clean geometric shapes (circles, arcs,
  rounded polygons, grids) with rounded corners, not sharp or hand-drawn organic blobs.
  _Why: it echoes the Rounded icons and the gently-rounded radius scale, so art reads as part of the
  same structured, modern system._
- **Flat and fill-led** — solid shapes over outline-led line art; depth comes from tonal steps of
  blue / teal, not from shadows or 3D rendering.
  _Why: flat, tonal artwork matches the subtle-depth, low-noise feel of the rest of the system._
