Dashboard

A page title and its description, sitting just below the topbar to open the screen. The description gives readers the one- or two-sentence orientation a bare title can't — what this page is for, what they can do here, and anything worth knowing before they start. It rides the quiet page-description type role and is capped to a comfortable reading measure, so it never sprawls across the full width; instead it wraps onto several lines and re-flows and mirrors with the rest of the page when the language switches.

Row header

A two-column label row — a fixed title column beside content that fills the rest.

Row header

This card sits in the showcase column of a page-row-header — the very two-column label row that lays out every specimen on this page, which is why it needs no standalone demo of its own. The row-header pairs a fixed-width title column with a content column that fills whatever inline space is left, and it stays flow-relative: switch to Arabic and the fixed column moves to the inline-start on the right without a single physical property changing. Drop as much content as you like into the filling column — a form, a table, a stack of cards, or a long passage of prose like this one — and it wraps and reflows within the remaining width while the label column holds its measure.

Main

The main column takes flex: 1 1 0 with a zero min-inline-size, so it fills whatever space the fixed sidebar leaves and can still shrink below its content's intrinsic width instead of forcing the shell to overflow.

Put the screen's primary content here — the sidebar carries navigation or filters at a steady width while this column carries the work. Under right-to-left the whole shell mirrors automatically, moving the sidebar to the inline-start on the right, because the layout leans on logical properties rather than left and right.
