Toggle

Four selection controls skinned onto native form elements — switch, checkbox, radio, and chip. Each keeps its own keyboard support and accessibility; nothing here is reimplemented.

Switch

A single on/off control — the input itself is the sliding track. Available in all five semantic color roles.

Checkbox

An independent yes/no selection, one or many at a time. Available in all five semantic color roles.

Radio

One choice from a mutually exclusive set. Available in all five semantic color roles.
