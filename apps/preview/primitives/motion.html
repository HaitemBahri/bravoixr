Motion

The raw motion primitives — a duration scale and named easing curves. Semantic motion tokens map onto these; nothing consumes them directly.

Duration scale

Raw time values, from instant to slower, that semantic duration roles map onto.

0ms --bravoixr-duration-instant
150ms --bravoixr-duration-fast
200ms --bravoixr-duration-base
300ms --bravoixr-duration-slow
500ms --bravoixr-duration-slower

Easing curves

Named cubic-bezier curves for motion timing. Hover a box to feel each curve.

--bravoixr-ease-decelerate
--bravoixr-ease-accelerate
