Semantic layout

Structural roles named for their use — control, card, modal — mapped onto the raw scale. Not themed: these hold across light and dark.

Radius roles

Corner-rounding steps mapped onto the elements they shape, from controls to pills.

--radius-control
--radius-card
--radius-modal
--radius-chip
--radius-pill

Border-width roles

Stroke weights for default and emphasized borders.

--border-width
--border-width-strong

Sizing roles

Fixed dimensions for recurring UI elements, like control height and the page's max width.

40px --control-height
1280px --container-max

Shadow roles (floating layers)

Elevation shadows for surfaces that float above the page, like menus and toasts.

--shadow-menu
--shadow-toast
--shadow-modal

Layer roles

Stacking-order tiers that keep floating layers in the right order.

0 --layer-base
10 --layer-sticky
20 --layer-dropdown
30 --layer-overlay
40 --layer-modal
50 --layer-popover
60 --layer-toast

Spacing seam (1:1 re-export)

The spacing scale re-exported 1:1 as a semantic role, unchanged from its primitive.
