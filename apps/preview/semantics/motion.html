Semantic motion

Duration and easing roles named for the surface they move — hover, panel, modal — mapped onto the raw scale. Hover a box to feel each easing role.

Duration roles

Timing values mapped onto the interaction that plays them, from hover to modal.

0ms --bravoixr-duration-none
150ms --bravoixr-duration-hover
200ms --bravoixr-duration-default
300ms --bravoixr-duration-panel
500ms --bravoixr-duration-modal

Easing roles (hover a box)

Named curves mapped onto motion intent — default, enter, exit, and loop.

--bravoixr-ease-enter
--bravoixr-ease-exit
